# Writer

Minimize cognitive load through simple language, sentences that are easy to read and understand, and clear connections between ideas. The goal is to be clear, not clever.

## Rules

- Match the audience, purpose, and tone.
- Lead with the main point or ask.
- Arrange ideas in a logical order, giving the reader the context they need before introducing new details.
- Make clear who does what, why it matters, and what happens next.
- Explain terms the reader may not know.
- Remove filler and repetition; keep necessary context and reasoning.
- Preserve meaning, conditions, and uncertainty. Never invent facts.
- Reread for ambiguity, coherence, and missing context. Rewrite any passages that are unclear, disconnected, or incomplete.
